# Cozy night and sound repair

Use the top-right time-of-day button to cycle **Daylight → Cozy night → Golden hour**. The scene transitions over roughly two seconds and preserves the selected mode when returning from the Panchayat. Rebuilding/customizing the city recreates lighting for the new scene.

Night includes warm/cool window variation with facade pane patterns, service-aware facility emissions, street and park lamps, bridge and beach promenade lighting, café festoons, an illuminated observation wheel, train interiors and headlights, car/bus headlights and rear lights, moving projected headlight pools, moonlight, stars, and subdued coastal water reflections. Instanced light pools supply wide coverage; six unshadowed accent lights illuminate key areas. Bloom is restrained, and ambient occlusion is disabled during night to avoid compounding the postprocessing cost. This is stylized lighting, not ray-traced illumination or physical reflections. Disaster-related service reduction continues to dim facility window emission.

Building clicks previously produced only a generic click with a small ambient-volume boost. They now trigger a separate three-second inspection cue with a quick attack and smooth release, normalized for audibility independently of zoom and ambient voice limits. Selecting another building fades the previous cue; stale async loads cannot replay an earlier selection. Music briefly ducks behind inspection/horn cues. Mute, hidden tabs and council entry cancel active cues.

Trains previously competed with cars and buses for a random short horn. They now reserve ambient voices, have a wider audible radius, and use a dedicated nearby-train trigger with per-train and global cooldowns. A locally bundled, CC0 Prime PM-920 locomotive air-horn recording replaces the generic road horn for trains. Its panner follows the moving train while audible. This is a locomotive-style horn chosen for recognizability, not an exact recording of the depicted metro. The procedural road horn remains a fallback if decoding fails. Source and edits are listed in `public/audio/credits.html`.

`window.vidyutCity.getAudio()` exposes output RMS, cue-only RMS, active cues and the last cue for browser verification. `getLighting()` reports mode, fade amount and lighting counts. `setLightingMode()` supports deterministic visual QA.

Checks: `tests/audio-mix.test.ts` covers priority voice reservation, train attenuation and bounded normalization. `scripts/night-audio-smoke.ts` checks night rendering, audible inspection cues, recorded train horn, day/golden/night transitions, council return, mobile and mute cleanup. Captures live under `experiments/results/cozy-night-*`; diagnostics are in `night-audio-smoke.json`. Automated checks measure signal and routing; subjective speaker/headphone quality still varies by device.
