Sounds of Nagar

The music, vehicle foley, utility sounds, footsteps, birds and interface tones are original procedural compositions generated locally by the application.

Human ambience

“Restaurant ambience” by stephan, PDSounds (2007), released into the public domain. Used as indistinct social ambience, not as spoken council dialogue. Edited to a 32-second mono excerpt, filtered, loudness adjusted, MP3 encoded and loop blended.

Source and public-domain dedication

Ocean

“Oceanwavescrushing” by Luftrum (2008), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported. Edited to a 40-second mono excerpt beginning at 12 seconds, filtered, loudness adjusted, MP3 encoded and loop blended.

Source and license · Original recording

“Prime PM-920” by HarveyHenkelmann (2009), dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. A 3.3-second excerpt beginning at 0.25 seconds, filtered, loudness adjusted, faded and encoded as mono MP3.
